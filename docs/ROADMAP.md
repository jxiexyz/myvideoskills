# Roadmap

1. **Completed — Core Cutout Runtime:** image loading, polygon cutout textures, pivot groups, nested parts, orthographic Three.js stage, keyframe timeline, and reusable manifests (`main-character` + `minimal-starter`).
2. **Completed — 5-Channel Animation & 16-Expression System:** cooperative `base` / `locomotion` / `head` / `gesture` / `face` channels, 45 reusable motions (including two-bone IK planted-foot walk/run), 16 expressions with clipped iris gaze, auto-blink, talking mouth & viseme hooks, and 7 combined presets.
3. **Completed — 4-Direction Multi-View Puppet System:** genuine layered directional SVG rigs for `front` (56 parts), `back` (22 parts), `top` (17 parts), and `bottom` (21 parts), `ViewAdapter` retargeting, synchronized mid-animation `setView()` switching, `4-View Sync` contact sheet, and active-view Rig Debug.
4. **Next — Extended Directional Packs:** `leftProfile`, `rightProfile`, `threeQuarterFrontLeft/Right`, and `threeQuarterBackLeft/Right` view packs plugging into `EXTENSIBLE_VIEW_SLOTS` and `character.registerView()`.
5. **Next — Authoring & Production Tools:** visual polygon/pivot editor, dedicated hand-swap artwork (e.g., `thumbsUp`, fist, peace sign), audio-driven phoneme lip-sync timeline, and sprite-sheet/video export.
